Chameleon

One phone each. Everyone gets the secret word except the chameleon, who has to bluff.

One person makes a code and reads it out. Everyone else types it in.

How to play

  1. Number yourselves clockwise around the circle. Everyone enters the same code, player count and category.
  2. Hold your card to see the secret word. The chameleon only sees the category.
  3. Starting with the player shown, everyone gives a one-word clue.
  4. When time's up, point at the chameleon on three. If they're caught, they get one guess at the word to win anyway.
