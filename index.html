    How to play Chameleon

    Everyone gets the same secret word except one person, the chameleon. Find the bluffer before they figure out the word.

    1. Join

    • The host taps Host a new game and reads the 4-letter code out loud.
    • Everyone else enters their name and the code. The host picks a category and timer, then starts.

    2. Check your card

    • Press and hold your card so nobody else can see it.
    • Everyone sees the category, a grid of 16 words, and the secret word highlighted.
    • The chameleon sees the category and the grid, but not which word it is.

    3. Give clues

    • Start with the person shown on screen, then go around your circle in whichever direction you like. Each person says one word related to the secret word.
    • Never say the secret word itself.
    • Too obvious, and the chameleon figures out the word. Too vague, and people think you're the chameleon.
    • The chameleon listens and bluffs a clue that fits in.

    4. Talk it over and point

    • When the timer runs out (or everyone has given a clue), discuss who seemed off.
    • On three, everyone points at who they think the chameleon is. The person with the most fingers is accused.
    • Tie? Quick re-vote between just the tied players. Still tied, the host decides.

    5. Reveal and who wins

    • The host taps Reveal and the answer shows on every phone.
    • Wrong person accused: the chameleon wins.
    • Chameleon caught: they get one guess at the word from the grid. Right, and the chameleon still wins. Wrong, and everyone else wins.
    No signal? Use "Play offline" on the start screen. Everyone enters the same code, player count and category, counts off clockwise, and player 1 runs the timer.
